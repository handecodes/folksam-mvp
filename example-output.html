AI Investment Analysis

This is what the real app renders for one case, captured here as a fixed example.

We want to build an AI chatbot that answers customer questions about their home and auto insurance policies, coverage details, and claims status, available on our website and app around the clock. It would use a large language model connected to our policy database and claims system through APIs, with a fallback to a human agent for anything it can't answer confidently. Estimated build cost is around 2 million SEK for a 4-month project, plus an ongoing licensing and hosting cost of roughly 300,000 SEK per year. We don't currently have anyone on the team with production experience running a customer-facing LLM system, though our engineers have general software development background.
